Health knowledge, simply.

General wellbeing guidance — not a treatment plan.

💧 Hydration

Drink safe water regularly. With vomiting or diarrhoea, oral rehydration solution (ORS) can replace lost fluids. Seek help for dehydration signs.

🥗 Balanced food

Choose varied meals with vegetables, fruit, grains and protein. Eat what you can tolerate and follow medical dietary advice.

🧼 Prevention

Wash hands, use safe food and water, keep vaccinations up to date and avoid close contact when unwell.

Medicine safety: The correct medicine depends on the confirmed cause, age, weight, allergies and other factors. Ask a qualified clinician or pharmacist. Never take antibiotics or prescription medicines based on this demo.
